Academic Writing

Elena’s scholarly work examines British Romantic poetry alongside transatlantic literary cultures and the forms and pressures that shape literary expression. Her articles appear in peer‑reviewed journals and contribute to ongoing conversations about Romanticism, narrative ambivalence and representation.

  • “Alien Mixtures: Health and Disease in The Sugar-Cane’s Biocolonial Ecosystem.” Early American Studies 24, no. 3 (2026): 286–311.
  • “Repetition and Clare’s Lyric of Withdrawn Revelation.” European Romantic Review 36, no. 1 (2025): 119–141.
  • “Misreading the Air: Narrative Ambivalence and the Burden of Representation in Dinaw Mengestu’s How to Read the Air.” ARIEL: A Review of International English Literature 55, nos. 3–4 (2024): 49–75.

Additional publications, conference papers and works‑in‑progress will be added as her research develops.
